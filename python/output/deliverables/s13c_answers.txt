Q1  Does speed change with distance to the nearest road?
   Evidence of an association (p=3.55e-32, effect=+0.003): speed INCREASES with distance from roads (animals are slower near roads).

Q2  Does the speed-distance relationship differ between urban and wildland?
   Evidence of an association (p=0.0057, effect=+0.009): the slope is HIGHER in urban areas than in wildland.

Q3  Does the speed-distance relationship differ between day and night?
   Evidence of an association (p=1.09e-52, effect=-0.007): the slope is LOWER by day than by night.

Q4a Do animals keep a different distance to roads in urban areas than in wildland?
   Evidence of an association (p=0, effect=-57.8%): animals are CLOSER to roads in urban areas.

Q4b Do animals keep a different distance to roads by day than by night?
   Evidence of an association (p=0.000369, effect=+3.6%): animals are FURTHER from roads by day.

Q4c Does the day-night difference in road distance depend on the landscape?
   Evidence of an association (p=1.12e-06, effect=+15.6%): the day-vs-night difference is LARGER (more positive) in urban areas.

MAIN Does the urban effect on the speed-distance slope differ between day and night?
   Evidence of an association (p=0.000842, effect=+0.021): the urban-vs-wildland slope difference is LARGER by day than by night.
